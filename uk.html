● FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I work while studying in the UK?

Yes. Most international students can work up to 20 hours per week during term time and full-time during vacations.

Do I need IELTS?

Many universities accept IELTS, PTE, TOEFL or Duolingo. Some universities also offer IELTS waivers.

Can I stay after graduation?

Yes. Under the Graduate Route Visa you can stay and work in the UK after completing your degree.
